Rules and Info

This board is an unofficial live tracker. Screenshots submitted to the mods are what count; the board fills in from TempleOSRS, and mods add what it can't see.

How to winEach tile is 1 point, and each completed line is 3 more. A line is any row, column or diagonal of 5 tiles. The team with the most points when time runs out wins. On a tie, the team that reached that score first wins.
TempleOSRS (optional)To show up here on your own, be in the Runecraft Bingo 2026 group on TempleOSRS (only a leader can add you, so ask a mod). Install the TempleOSRS RuneLite plugin and, in its settings, turn on Automatically sync Collection Log (Collection Log section) and Auto-Update (Ranks section). In the game settings, turn on the Collection log new addition chat message. Then open your collection log and click through every page once.
First-time dropsIf the item is new to your collection log, it reaches the board on its own, as long as Automatically sync Collection Log and the new addition chat message are on.
Repeat dropsIf you already had the item, the board only sees your count go up. Open that item's collection log page after the drop so Temple picks up the new count. If you're unsure, open it anyway.
Drop timesA drop is timed to when your collection log sent it to TempleOSRS, so open the page right after the drop. Mods can correct the time from your screenshot. Drops from before the end that sync late still count for 3 hours after it.
KC and XPProgress charts and the Runecraft tile use your TempleOSRS stats, which Auto-Update refreshes when you log out. On long grinds, log out now and then.
ScreenshotsScreenshot every bingo drop with the Clan Events plugin on and submit it to the mods. Screenshots are what count; the board just tracks progress live.
Not using TempleOSRS?Your drops won't show here on their own. Submit your screenshots and a mod adds them to the board.
